# ETF guide source review, 2026-10-16

Scope: four education guides, no fund recommendation or live quote claims. Official original HTML bodies fetched and directly read during this checkpoint, not search snippets alone.

1. https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-24
   - SEC staff ETF bulletin. Read body sections on NAV, exchange trading, authorized participants, arbitrage, premiums/discounts, spread definition and investor checks.
   - Supports expense/spread/NAV mechanics. Limited to registered investment-company ETFs, not all ETPs. Expected arbitrage alignment is not a guarantee.
2. https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/mutual-fund-and-etf-fees-and-expenses-investor-bulletin
   - July 23, 2025 SEC staff fees bulletin. Read fee-table, operating-cost, zero-expense, ETF transaction-cost, waiver and acquired-fund expense sections.
   - Supports ratio as percentage of average net assets, costs paid from assets, missing investor-level costs, waiver/recoupment caveat. No fund-specific current fee asserted.
3. https://www.investor.gov/additional-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletin-index-funds
   - SEC staff index-funds bulletin. Read replication/sampling/derivatives, costs and risks sections.
   - Supports imperfect tracking and fee/trading/sampling effects. Does NOT establish a universal standard-deviation formula. Guide explicitly labels its statistical convention and annualization assumptions.
   - Historical Form N-Q reference in the source is not reused as a current reporting requirement.
