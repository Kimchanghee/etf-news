1. https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-24

   - SEC staff index-funds bulletin. Read replication/sampling/derivatives, costs and risks sections.
   - Supports imperfect tracking and fee/trading/sampling effects. Does NOT establish a universal standard-deviation formula. Guide explicitly labels its statistical convention and annualization assumptions.
